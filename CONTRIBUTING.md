# Contributing

## Bugs and fixes: yes, please

If a relic misbehaves, open an issue. Useful reports say what you asked Claude, what it did, and what you expected. Your OS and whether you installed via the marketplace or by hand both help.

Pull requests that fix a bug, a broken path, or a typo are welcome.

## New relics: curated, not open

AIR is a curated shelf rather than a collection. Each relic is here because it solved a real problem in real work and kept earning its place, and that filter is the whole value. So new relics come from [Gyozan](https://www.gyozan.com) rather than from pull requests.

If you have built something you think belongs here, open an issue describing what it grants and what it is grounded in. That is a conversation worth having, it just is not a merge.

## If you fork it

The relics are MIT, so build on them freely. The AIR name, logo and brand artwork are not part of that license, so give your version its own name.
